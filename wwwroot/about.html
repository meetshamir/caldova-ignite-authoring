About Caldova

Caldova Retail Co. designs and sells gear for trail, camp, and everyday adventure. Founded by a small crew of trail runners and weekend campers, we focus on durable, well-made basics rather than chasing every seasonal trend.

Caldova Retail Ops is the storefront and operations dashboard our team uses to track orders, catalog, and daily sales.
